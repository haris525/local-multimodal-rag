# global-5

Question: Which Articles of the original 1787 Constitution are divided into numbered Sections, how many Sections does each of them have, and how many Sections are there in all?

Gold answer: Four of the seven Articles. Article 1 has 10 Sections, Article 2 has 4, Article 3 has 3 and Article 4 has 4, 21 Sections in all. Articles Five, Six and Seven are not divided into Sections.

Documents: us-constitution-1787.txt

## Arithmetic and notes

A search for lines starting with "Section" finds 21 headings: Sections 1 to 10 of Article 1 (lines 14, 18, 51, 89, 99, 120, 135, 166, 229, 260), 1 to 4 of Article 2 (282, 347, 371, 381), 1 to 3 of Article 3 (387, 394, 416) and 1 to 4 of Article 4 (429, 435, 450, 462). The only other "Section" is Article V's reference to "the ninth Section of the first Article" (line 479). 10 + 4 + 3 + 4 = 21. The Article headings are written "Article 1", "ARTICLE 2", then "ARTICLE THREE" to "ARTICLE SEVEN".

## What makes it global

Counting needs every Section heading in the document, from line 14 to line 462, plus the observation that the last three Articles have none; no passage gives these numbers, so a few retrieved chunks cannot.
