# global-16

Question: Each of these three papers fine-tunes at least one pretrained model. For each paper, name every model it fine-tunes (the base model), what the result is used for, the data it is trained on, and the number of epochs and the learning rate where the paper gives them.

Gold answer: Gorilla: LLaMA-7B, instruction-fine-tuned (with and without a retriever) into Gorilla, on APIBench: 16,450 instruction-API pairs that GPT-4 generated by self-instruct for 1,645 APIs (90/10 train/evaluation split for HuggingFace, 80/20 for Torch Hub and TensorFlow Hub); 5 epochs, learning rate 2e-5 with cosine decay, batch size 64, on 8 A100 40GB GPUs (Appendix 8.2, Table 4). Best-practices RAG: (a) Llama-2-7B as the RAG generator, fine-tuned with LoRA and int8 quantization on the train splits of ASQA, HotpotQA, NarrativeQA, NQ, SQuAD, TriviaQA and TruthfulQA, with gold and random contexts mixed (the Mgr variant works best); 3 epochs, learning rate 5e-5, batch size 4, maximum length 1600 (Appendix A.5); (b) BERT-base-multilingual-cased as the query classifier that decides whether retrieval is needed, trained on a Databricks-Dolly-15K subset plus GPT-4-generated data, batch size 16, learning rate 1e-5 (Appendix A.1; no epoch count given). Mistral 7B: the base Mistral 7B fine-tuned into Mistral 7B - Instruct on instruction datasets publicly available on the Hugging Face repository, with no proprietary data or training tricks; the paper gives no epochs or learning rate.

Documents: gorilla-llm-api-calls-2023.pdf, rag-best-practices-2024.pdf, mistral-7b-2023.pdf

## Arithmetic and notes

Searches for finetun, fine-tun, train, epoch and learning rate in each paper find no other model trained by the authors and no Mistral hyperparameters ("epoch" and "learning rate" occur only on Gorilla p13-14 and best practices p19 and p21). The best-practices reranking section (p8) explains how DLM rerankers are fine-tuned in general but uses the existing monoT5, monoBERT, RankLLaMA and TILDEv2 implementations (PyGaggle, TILDE) with no training setup of its own, so they are not in the gold list; an answer that adds them is not penalized. "Trivi- aQA" is a line-break hyphen; the PDF writes the Mistral and Llama 2 chat model names with en dashes.

## What makes it global

The base models are in the main text but the data splits, epochs and learning rates are only in the appendices (Gorilla p13-14, best practices p19-21), and the best-practices paper fine-tunes two models in two different sections (query classification and generator). A few retrieved chunks give the base models and miss the classifier or the hyperparameters.
