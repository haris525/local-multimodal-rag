# global-4

Question: In how many Sections of the original 1787 Constitution text does treason come up? For each, give the Article and Section and what the text says about treason there.

Gold answer: Four Sections, one in each of the first four Articles. Article I, Section 6: Senators and Representatives are privileged from arrest while attending Congress and going to and from it, except for treason, felony and breach of the peace. Article II, Section 4: the President, Vice President and all civil officers are removed from office on impeachment for, and conviction of, treason, bribery or other high crimes and misdemeanors. Article III, Section 3: treason against the United States consists only in levying war against them or adhering to their enemies, giving them aid and comfort; no one is convicted of treason without the testimony of two witnesses to the same overt act or a confession in open court; Congress declares the punishment, but no attainder of treason works corruption of blood or forfeiture except during the life of the person attainted. Article IV, Section 2: a person charged in any State with treason, felony or other crime who flees to another State is delivered up on demand of the executive authority of the State he fled, to be removed to the State with jurisdiction.

## Arithmetic and notes

A case-insensitive search for "treason" finds seven occurrences on lines 122, 383, 416, 418, 422, 423 and 438, which belong to exactly four Sections (I.6, II.4, III.3, IV.2), one in each of the first four Articles.

## What makes it global

Only Article III, Section 3 is about treason as such; the other three mentions are single words inside clauses about Congress, impeachment and extradition, spread from line 122 to line 438. Searching for treason returns the definition first and easily stops there.
